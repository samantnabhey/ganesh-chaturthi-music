About this Ganpati playlist

What is Ganpati Bappa Music?

It's a browser-based Ganesh Chaturthi music player built for quick, uninterrupted listening without registration.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The player is designed for mobile screens, with large play, skip and seek controls for easy use.

Where does the music play from?

The songs play through YouTube. You can also open the full playlist directly on YouTube Music.
